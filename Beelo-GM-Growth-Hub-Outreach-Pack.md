## 3. One-page pilot brief

### The proposition

**Beelo is an offline-first operational-memory layer for self-employed field professionals working across disconnected company and consumer tools.**

It is initially focused on commission-based, home-visit window-coverings advisors. This narrow starting group has a repeated workflow: receive a lead, contact the customer, travel, advise or measure, quote, revisit or fit, follow up, record expenses and reconcile commission.

### The problem

Customer appointments, conversations, routes, photographs, receipts, mileage and earnings records live in separate systems. The worker must reconstruct the context manually, often while travelling or working in a customer's home. This creates:

- slow or missed customer follow-up;
- lost promises and job context;
- unrealistic scheduling and avoidable knock-on delays;
- incomplete mileage and expense records;
- poor visibility of commission deductions; and
- unpaid evening administration and cognitive load.

### The product

Beelo is being rebuilt as a secure progressive web app with offline-capable core workflows. It is intended to:

- capture voice notes, photographs and screenshots;
- keep information connected to the relevant customer and visit;
- resurface next-job context and outstanding actions;
- draft communications for the user's review and approval;
- flag potential schedule risk; and
- create export-ready mileage, expense and earnings records.

Beelo is not a CRM, accounting or tax-filing product. It does not replace a franchisor's booking, pricing, ordering or fulfilment systems.

### Responsible-AI principles

- Human approval before any customer communication.
- No autonomous booking, financial or customer decisions.
- Clear separation between captured facts, calculated information and AI suggestions.
- Source visibility, correction and deletion for important records.
- Data minimisation and defined retention for pilot information.
- Confidence and exception handling for document extraction.
- Core access remains useful when connectivity is poor.

### Proposed pilot

**Cohort:** 5–10 self-employed home-visit advisors<br>
**Indicative length:** 6–8 weeks after onboarding<br>
**Method:** baseline week, structured onboarding, live use, weekly check-ins and closing interviews

The pilot should answer three questions:

1. Does Beelo solve a frequent and costly operational problem?
2. Which workflows produce enough value to support repeated use and payment?
3. Can those workflows be delivered safely and reliably in real field conditions?

### Measures

Use a small, credible primary set rather than claiming every possible benefit.

**Primary measures**

- Percentage of new leads receiving first contact within the user's target time.
- Percentage of recorded follow-up actions completed by the due time.
- Self-reported weekly administration time.
- Weekly active use and retention through the pilot.

**Secondary measures**

- Completeness of mileage and expense records.
- Number and usefulness of schedule-risk warnings.
- Accuracy and correction rate of extracted document fields.
- Number of commission discrepancies identified or explained.
- User confidence, perceived cognitive load and willingness to pay.

No productivity or financial benefit should be advertised as proven until pilot evidence supports it.

## 4. Twenty-minute meeting structure

**0–3 minutes — founder and problem**<br>
Explain how the product arose from doing the job, not from observing the sector at a distance.

**3–7 minutes — demonstration**<br>
Show one end-to-end workflow only: new lead or appointment → captured context → next action → user-approved follow-up.

**7–11 minutes — evidence and boundaries**<br>
Describe the two months of personal field use, what remains unproven, and what Beelo deliberately does not replace.

**11–15 minutes — pilot**<br>
Present the cohort, 6–8 week structure, four primary measures and responsible-AI controls.

**15–20 minutes — specific requests**<br>
Ask for a named adviser, pilot-design feedback, relevant introductions and a funding-readiness route.

### Closing question

“Based on Beelo's current stage, what evidence or technical work would you want to see before recommending it for an innovation programme, university collaboration or funding opportunity?”
