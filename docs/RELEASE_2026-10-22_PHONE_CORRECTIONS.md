# Append-only phone-record corrections

Visit → Phone contact / history → Recorded phone contact now exposes Correct record on active adviser-recorded outcomes. Confirmation explains that the record, not the real call, is being retracted. Cancel changes nothing. Confirm appends a timestamped correction referencing the original ID; it never edits/deletes the original or changes booking/sent flags. A correct replacement outcome can be recorded separately. Complete history is accessible, not just the last five items, and retracted rows remain labelled.

Shared phone evidence ignores retracted records. Still-needed pre-visit reminders derive again, while independent valid phone records and real sent flags continue to fulfill their own purposes. Cancelled/ended visits do not regain obsolete pre-visit tasks. Historical completed task records are not rewritten; any returning reminder is derived from current evidence. Correction is allowed for historical visits, without allowing retrospective new call assertions. Atomic writes validate targets, reject missing/non-phone targets and deduplicate repeated correction operations and already-retracted targets.

Testing uncovered offline banner/toast interception of the confirmation control. Only overlays containing the correction sheet are raised above those notifications; global modal behaviour is unchanged. Save errors render inline with an alert role so they remain visible at that stacking level. Browser hit-testing covers top/middle/bottom of the button. Simulated write failure retains history and allows retry.
