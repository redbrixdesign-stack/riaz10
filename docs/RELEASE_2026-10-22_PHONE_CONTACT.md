# Adviser-recorded phone contact

Follow-ups pre-visit cards offer Record phone contact under More options. Visit details offer Phone contact / history even after a reminder clears. Four explicit outcomes: introduced myself; visit date/time agreed; both; no answer/voicemail. Saving records an enumerated local adviser assertion with timestamp, customer identity, booking snapshot, channel and provenance. It does not place a call, send a message, write a WhatsApp communication or set introSent/dayBeforeSent.

Introduction fulfills only introduction; date/slot agreement fulfills only confirmation; both can clear the combined purpose. No answer fulfills neither. Shared Follow-ups/Messages review rules respect this evidence. Snoozed durable reminders resolve with phone provenance when their purpose is fulfilled, preserving records/history. Unrelated tasks, service issues and outcome logging remain separate.

Agreement applies only to the recorded date/type/arrival slot/schedule revision and customer. Changed booking requires fresh agreement; introduction can remain for that customer. Reassignment does not transfer phone evidence. Database writes recheck the booking snapshot/status inside a transaction, reject stale forms/invalid outcomes and deduplicate repeated operation IDs. History is retained within the appointment and therefore follows its existing backup/restore path; no schema migration or free-text PII field added.
